## THE PROBLEM

Your workflow (`Create App → Create Datasource → Create Schema → Create Pages → Add Navigation`) is technically **excellent** but prevents 95% of your target market from using AppBana.

**You're serving developers (5% of market) but losing operations managers, business analysts, and IT managers (95% of market).**

---

## THE SOLUTION

Implement **3-Tier User Experience** over 45-60 days:

### Tier 1: Template-Driven (70% of users)
- Pre-built templates (Equipment Tracking, Patient Management, etc.)
- Working app in 5-15 minutes
- Targets: Operations managers, business users

### Tier 2: Guided Wizard (25% of users)
- "Define data → Auto-generate pages" workflow
- Working app in 30-60 minutes
- Targets: IT managers, power business users

### Tier 3: Power User (5% of users)
- Your current workflow (unchanged)
- Full control and flexibility
- Targets: Developers, ISVs, consultants

---

## THE 5 CRITICAL GAPS

| Gap | Impact | Solution | Time |
|-----|--------|----------|------|
| **1. Auto-Page Generation** | HIGHEST | Schema → 4 working pages (1 sec) | Week 1-2 |
| **2. Template System** | HIGH | 5 pre-built industry templates | Week 3-4 |
| **3. Entity Abstraction** | MEDIUM | Hide SQL behind business concepts | Week 2-3 |
| **4. Schema-to-UI Sync** | MEDIUM | Forms auto-update when schema changes | Week 2 |
| **5. AI/Conversational** | LOWER | Natural language → schema (optional) | Week 6-8 |
